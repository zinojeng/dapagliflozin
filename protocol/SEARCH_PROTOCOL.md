# Search protocol

Search end date: 2026-08-27

Databases and sources:
- PubMed/MEDLINE
- Crossref
- ClinicalTrials.gov
- official journal websites
- KDIGO, ADA, ESC, ACC/AHA and regulatory labels
- citation chaining from pivotal trials

Main domains:
1. Type 2 diabetes and cardiovascular prevention
2. HFrEF, HFmrEF, HFpEF
3. Acute heart-failure hospitalization
4. CKD with and without diabetes
5. Post-TAVI and post-MI
6. Advanced CKD, dialysis and kidney transplantation
7. ADPKD
8. Safety, ketoacidosis, perioperative care and sick-day rules
9. Combination cardiorenal therapy

Date windows:
- Landmark evidence: no date restriction
- Frontier evidence: 2024-01-01 through search end date
- Case reports: 2023 through search end date

Required outputs:
- trial characteristics matrix
- endpoint/component matrix
- evidence certainty
- applicability and exclusion criteria
- unresolved questions
