# Frontier and evidence cliffs — dapagliflozin, as of search end date 2026-08-27

Maintained by: dapa-frontier-evidence
An "evidence cliff" = the boundary where randomized clinical-outcome evidence ends and practice must rely on surrogates, subgroups, observational data, or extrapolation.

---

## 1. Structural heart disease (post-TAVI)

**Frontier established.** DapaTAVI (NEJM 2025; DOI 10.1056/NEJMoa2500366) is the first RCT showing clinical-event benefit of an SGLT2i started after TAVI in high-HF-risk aortic stenosis patients: death/worsening HF HR 0.72 (0.55–0.95), driven by worsening HF (subHR 0.63, 0.45–0.88).
**Cliff:** (a) all-comer TAVI patients without prior HF admission — untested; (b) severe AS *before* valve intervention — untested (SGLT2i trials excluded significant valvular disease); (c) open-label design and Spain-only setting limit generalizability; (d) no incremental health-status (KCCQ) benefit — symptom claims are unsupported (JACC 2025; DOI 10.1016/j.jacc.2025.07.051).

## 2. Acute HF — in-hospital initiation

**Frontier:** DAPA ACT HF-TIMI 68 (Circulation 2025; DOI 10.1161/CIRCULATIONAHA.125.076575) — the dedicated dapagliflozin trial was **neutral on its primary** (HR 0.86, 0.68–1.08, P=0.20) over a 2-month window; safety of in-hospital initiation confirmed (hypotension 3.6% vs 2.2%; worsening kidney function 5.9% vs 4.7%). Class-level meta-analyses (embedded prespecified meta: CV death/worsening HF HR 0.71, 0.54–0.93; J Card Fail 2026 TSA meta: all-cause death RR 0.61, 0.47–0.81, TSA-firm) support early initiation as a class.
**Cliff:** (a) no *dapagliflozin-specific* proof of early-initiation outcome benefit — the evidence is class-extrapolated (a dapagliflozin-specific care-spectrum meta-analysis of DAPA-HF/DELIVER/DAPA ACT HF-TIMI 68 is in press — Berg et al., JACC Heart Fail 2026;14(8):103232, DOI 10.1016/j.jchf.2026.103232 — but no results are retrievable yet; cite existence only); (b) ≤2-month follow-up in all in-hospital trials; (c) cardiogenic shock/inotrope-dependent, and critically ill patients excluded; (d) optimal timing (first 24 h vs stabilized) still undefined — DICTATE-AHF (24-h initiation) missed its primary diuretic-efficiency endpoint (OR 0.65, 0.41–1.02, P=0.06; JACC 2024; DOI 10.1016/j.jacc.2024.02.009).

## 3. Post-MI

**Frontier:** DAPA-MI (NEJM Evid 2024; DOI 10.1056/EVIDoa2300286): cardiometabolic benefit only (WR 1.34, 1.20–1.50; driven by new-onset T2DM prevention and weight); CV death/HHF HR 0.95 (0.64–1.40).
**Cliff:** no evidence that dapagliflozin improves hard CV outcomes after MI in patients without diabetes/HF/CKD; consistent with class signal in EMPACT-MI (empagliflozin, neutral primary). Post-MI prescription for cardiovascular protection per se is **not** evidence-based; prescription to prevent incident T2DM in prediabetic post-MI patients is a defensible, secondary-analysis-supported (not guideline-mandated) use (JAHA 2025; DOI 10.1161/JAHA.124.040327).

## 4. Advanced CKD (eGFR <20–25, pre-dialysis)

**Cliff (still standing, decision pending):** DAPA-CKD enrolled down to eGFR 25 (mean benefit consistent in low-eGFR subgroup — landmark peer's domain). Below eGFR 20–25 there is **no randomized outcome evidence**. Renal Lifecycle (NCT05374291; design NDT 2025, DOI 10.1093/ndt/gfaf046) is the dedicated event-driven RCT (eGFR ≤25, dialysis, transplant; primary HHF/all-cause death/kidney failure). **No primary results identified through 2026-08-27** — this is the single most practice-relevant pending readout in the field. KDIGO 2024 supports continuing SGLT2i until dialysis/transplant; initiation below eGFR 20 remains extrapolation.

## 5. Dialysis

**Frontier:** DARE-ESKD-2 (Kidney Int Rep 2026; DOI 10.1016/j.ekir.2026.106355): safe over 24 wk in 80 patients, but neutral on NT-proBNP and all functional/echo surrogates. DARE-ESKD-1 (CJASN 2023; DOI 10.2215/CJN.0000000000000196): favorable PK — non-accumulating, ~0.1% dialyzed out.
**Supporting-but-weak:** DAPA-CKD dialysis-initiation post hoc (CKJ 2026; DOI 10.1093/ckj/sfag170): all-cause mortality aHR 0.47 (0.23–0.98), driven by non-CV death — observational-grade.
**Cliff:** no randomized clinical-outcome evidence in prevalent dialysis; mechanism itself questionable with minimal residual filtration (off-target/cardiac effects hypothesized, unproven [mech]). Renal Lifecycle dialysis stratum + STOP-HF-in-PD will address.

## 6. Kidney transplantation

**Frontier:** INFINITI (CJASN 2026, online 2025-12-12; PMID 41385300): first double-blind RCT physiology data — GFR dip preserved (−4.2 mL/min/1.73 m² wk 1), natriuresis and BP effects muted, clean 12-wk safety (no rejection, stable tacrolimus).
**Cliff:** zero randomized clinical-outcome evidence; immunosuppression-related infection concerns (genital mycotic/UTI) not excluded by a 52-patient 12-week trial. Renal Lifecycle transplant stratum (eGFR ≤45) is the pending outcome test.

## 7. ADPKD

**Frontier:** one small crossover RCT (Uchiyama, KI Rep 2025; DOI 10.1016/j.ekir.2025.01.023; n=27 on tolvaptan): eGFR slope +2.57 vs −5.65 mL/min/1.73 m²/yr (P=0.002), TKV −0.44% vs +5.04% (P=0.01) — surrogate, short, open-label.
**Cliff:** ADPKD was excluded from DAPA-CKD/EMPA-KIDNEY; preclinical concern (vasopressin rise → cystogenesis) unresolved; observational signals favorable but confounded (TriNetX HR 0.35, 0.21–0.61 for dialysis initiation — Mayo Clin Proc IQO 2026). No adequately powered outcome RCT exists or has reported; dedicated programs are being organized per Müller et al. NDT 2025 (DOI 10.1093/ndt/gfaf061). Clinical use in ADPKD remains off-evidence.

## 8. HFpEF/HFmrEF chronic care, diabetes prevention, combination therapy

- Chronic HF across LVEF: landmark territory (DAPA-HF/DELIVER — landmark peer). Frontier additions are pooled/post hoc refinements only (e.g., recent-hospitalization DELIVER/EMPEROR-Preserved pooling, Front Cardiovasc Med 2026, exploratory).
- Combination cardiorenal therapy frontier: MIRO-CKD phase 2b (balcinrenone/dapagliflozin; NDT 2025, DOI 10.1093/ndt/gfaf119) — UACR surrogate; no outcome data.

---

## Ranked evidence cliffs (most practice-relevant first)

1. **Advanced CKD/dialysis/transplant outcomes** — Renal Lifecycle readout pending; currently extrapolation-only below eGFR 20–25 and beyond KRT boundaries.
2. **Dapagliflozin-specific early-initiation benefit in acute HF** — class-level meta only; dedicated trial neutral.
3. **ADPKD** — surrogate-only RCT evidence, n=27.
4. **Post-MI hard outcomes** — tested and not shown (this is a "negative-evidence floor," not a data gap: do not claim benefit).
5. **All-comer post-TAVI and pre-intervention severe AS** — benefit shown only in high-HF-risk post-TAVI patients.
