# Ongoing / pending dapagliflozin trials relevant to the review (as of 2026-08-27)

Maintained by: dapa-frontier-evidence
Status labels: **ongoing** (recruiting/follow-up), **fully recruited**, **results pending** (completed or event-driven, no primary publication found by search end date), **design-published**.

---

## 1. Renal Lifecycle — NCT05374291 — **results pending / ongoing; highest priority**

- Population: ≈1500 planned (imaging-substudy paper cites ≈1750): eGFR ≤25 mL/min/1.73 m², on HD/PD, or kidney transplant recipients with eGFR ≤45.
- Design: pragmatic, international, investigator-initiated, double-blind, placebo-controlled; dapagliflozin 10 mg/d; event-driven (468 first primary events; 80% power, 25% RRR, assumed 12.5%/yr event rate).
- Primary: HF hospitalization, all-cause mortality, or (non-dialysis) kidney failure.
- Secondaries include per-component and per-stratum (advanced CKD / dialysis / transplant) analyses.
- Verification: systematic search through 2026-08-27 found **no primary-results publication or full presentation**; the trial's own cardiac-imaging substudy design paper (Clin Kidney J 2026; DOI 10.1093/ckj/sfaf376) still frames CV protection in this population as unestablished.
- Sources: design — Bakker WM et al. NDT 2025, DOI 10.1093/ndt/gfaf046, PMID 40053493; substudies — Karsten M et al. CKJ 2026, DOI 10.1093/ckj/sfaf376, PMID 41551843.
- Action for Director: monitor ASN Kidney Week / ERA 2026–2027; this readout can move guideline boundaries for advanced CKD, dialysis, and transplant simultaneously.

## 2. Renal Lifecycle cardiac-imaging substudies — **ongoing; surrogate**

- STOP-HF-in-PD: echo substudy, n=100 PD patients; primary LV global longitudinal strain at 6 mo.
- Cardiac MRI substudy: n=250 across all three strata; primary indexed LV mass at 12 mo.
- Both are surrogate mechanistic substudies — will not by themselves justify outcome claims.
- Source: Karsten M et al. CKJ 2026, DOI 10.1093/ckj/sfaf376, PMID 41551843. [design-published]

## 3. MIRO-CKD — NCT06350123 — **fully recruited; phase 2b surrogate**

- Balcinrenone/dapagliflozin (15/10, 40/10) vs dapagliflozin 10 mg in CKD, eGFR 25–60; n=324 randomized across 15 countries; primary log-UACR change at week 12 + 8-wk washout.
- Relevance: combination cardiorenal therapy domain; dose-finding for phase 3.
- Source: Mark PB et al. NDT 2025, DOI 10.1093/ndt/gfaf119, PMID 40623005.

## 4. ADPKD trial programs — **design/organizational stage**

- Müller RU et al. (NDT 2025, DOI 10.1093/ndt/gfaf061, PMID 40199734) catalogue running/upcoming SGLT2i-in-ADPKD programs across regions; as of search end date no adequately powered dapagliflozin outcome RCT in ADPKD has reported.
- Empagliflozin feasibility RCT (n=50, 1-yr, safety/tolerability primary; Seliger SL et al., DOI 10.1159/000549447, PMID 41505371) — class-relevant, not dapagliflozin; ongoing.
- Only reported dapagliflozin RCT remains the n=27 crossover (Uchiyama, KI Rep 2025, DOI 10.1016/j.ekir.2025.01.023) — surrogate endpoints.

## 5. Heart-transplant renal protection — NCT05321706 — **ongoing**

- "DAPAgliflozin for Renal Protection in Heart Transplant Recipients" (registry listing; no results publication found in window). Peripheral to the review's core domains; track only.

## 6. SGLT2i effects on kidney function in advanced CKD — NCT06982079 — **ongoing**

- ClinicalTrials.gov listing surfaced in searches (class-level, advanced CKD kidney-function endpoints); no publications yet. Track; verify agent and design against the registry before citing in synthesis.

## 7. Acute-HF space — no further dapagliflozin outcome RCT identified

- After DAPA ACT HF-TIMI 68 (completed, published Circulation 2025) and DICTATE-AHF (published JACC 2024), searches through 2026-08-27 identified **no new dedicated dapagliflozin acute-HF outcome trial**. The open question (longer-horizon early-initiation benefit) is currently addressed only by class-level meta-analysis (J Card Fail 2026 TSA meta, DOI 10.1016/j.cardfail.2026.04.018: "further evidence from large-scale RCTs with longer follow-ups is required").
