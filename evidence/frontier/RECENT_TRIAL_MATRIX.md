## 1. DapaTAVI — dapagliflozin after TAVI

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Registration | NCT04696185 |
| Design | Investigator-initiated, pragmatic, randomized, controlled, **open-label** (PROBE), multicentre (Spain); dapagliflozin 10 mg/d vs standard care alone after TAVI |
| Population | Older adults with severe aortic stenosis undergoing TAVI, prior HF admission plus ≥1 of: renal insufficiency (eGFR 25–75), diabetes, or LVEF ≤40% |
| N | 620 dapagliflozin vs 637 standard care randomized; 1222 in primary analysis |
| Primary endpoint | All-cause death or worsening HF (hospitalization or urgent visit) at 1 year |
| Primary result [RCT-primary] | 15.0% vs 20.1%; HR 0.72 (95% CI 0.55–0.95), P=0.02 |
| Components | All-cause death: 7.8% vs 8.9%, HR 0.87 (0.59–1.28) — **not individually significant**. Worsening HF: 9.4% vs 14.4%, subhazard ratio 0.63 (0.45–0.88) — **the driver** (see COMPOSITE_ENDPOINT_DECOMPOSITION.md) |
| Safety | Genital infection and hypotension significantly more common with dapagliflozin |
| Publication | Raposeiras-Roubín S et al. N Engl J Med 2025. DOI 10.1056/NEJMoa2500366. PMID 40162639 |
| Status | **Peer-reviewed full publication** |
| Caveats | Open-label design (endpoint adjudication blinded); Spain-only; population selected for high HF risk — not all-comer TAVI |

Related analyses:
- Design paper: Amat-Santos IJ et al. Eur J Heart Fail 2022. DOI 10.1002/ejhf.2370. PMID 34693613. [protocol]
- KCCQ health status, **prespecified**: no additional KCCQ benefit over TAVI itself (3-mo OR for improvement 0.96, 95% CI 0.72–1.26, P=0.745; 12-mo OR 1.03, 0.83–1.27, P=0.819; n=964 with baseline KCCQ). Bonanad-Lozano C et al. J Am Coll Cardiol 2025. DOI 10.1016/j.jacc.2025.07.051. PMID 41062227. [prespec][surrogate]
- Win-ratio re-analysis, **post hoc methodological**: WR 1.36 (1.03–1.78), P=.028 for original primary; adding KCCQ to the hierarchy shifts estimate to null (WR 1.10, 0.94–1.30). Rossello X et al. Rev Esp Cardiol (Engl Ed) 2026. DOI 10.1016/j.rec.2025.08.003. PMID 40976500. [post hoc]

## 2. DAPA ACT HF-TIMI 68 — in-hospital initiation in acute HF

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Registration | NCT04363697 |
| Design | International, randomized, **double-blind**, placebo-controlled; in-hospital initiation of dapagliflozin 10 mg/d; blinded treatment for 2 months |
| Population | 2401 patients hospitalized for acute HF (any LVEF; 71.5% LVEF ≤40%; 44.7% de novo HF; median age 69; 33.9% women; 18.7% Black) |
| N | 1218 dapagliflozin vs 1183 placebo (randomized Sep 2020–Mar 2025) |
| Primary endpoint | CV death or worsening HF (in-hospital worsening, rehospitalization for HF, urgent HF visit) through 2 months |
| Primary result [RCT-primary] | 10.9% vs 12.7%; HR 0.86 (95% CI 0.68–1.08), **P=0.20 — NOT statistically significant** |
| Secondary/components | Worsening HF event: 9.4% vs 10.3%, HR 0.91 (0.71–1.18). CV death: 2.5% vs 3.1%, HR 0.78 (0.48–1.27). All-cause death: 3.0% vs 4.5%, HR 0.66 (0.43–1.00) — secondary outcome, borderline, hypothesis-generating only |
| Safety | Symptomatic hypotension 3.6% vs 2.2%; worsening kidney function 5.9% vs 4.7% |
| Embedded prespecified meta-analysis | SGLT2i initiated in patients hospitalized for HF (trial-level, incl. EMPULSE/SOLOIST-WHF-type trials; n≈3527): CV death/worsening HF HR 0.71 (0.54–0.93), P=0.012; all-cause death HR 0.57 (0.41–0.80), P=0.001. **Class-level meta-analytic estimate, not a dapagliflozin primary result** |
| Publication | Berg DD et al. Circulation 2025. DOI 10.1161/CIRCULATIONAHA.125.076575. PMID 40884036. PMCID PMC12910484 |
| Status | **Peer-reviewed full publication** (presented ESC 2025) |
| Caveats | Short 2-month blinded window; event rates lower than projected; do not describe as "dapagliflozin reduces mortality in acute HF" — the primary endpoint was missed |

Related:
- Design paper: Berg DD et al. JACC Heart Fail 2025. DOI 10.1016/j.jchf.2025.03.014. PMID 40335233. [protocol]
- Viewpoint: Prado-Prado H, Raposeiras-Roubín S. Eur Cardiol Rev 2026. DOI 10.15420/ecr.2025.70. PMID 41837183. [author interpretation]

## 3. 2026 in-hospital/care-spectrum meta-analysis (acute HF initiation)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Best match found | Ahmed M et al. "In-Hospital Initiation of SGLT2 Inhibitors in Patients with Acute Heart Failure: A Meta-analysis of RCTs with Trial Sequential Analysis." J Card Fail 2026. DOI 10.1016/j.cardfail.2026.04.018. PMID 42067122 |
| Scope | 8 RCTs, n=4096, in-hospital initiation (dapagliflozin, empagliflozin, sotagliflozin); weighted median follow-up 60 days; search through 2025-09-10 (includes DAPA ACT HF-TIMI 68) |
| Results | All-cause death RR 0.61 (95% CI 0.47–0.81); worsening HF RR 0.67 (0.48–0.94); CV death RR 0.68 (0.47–0.99); HF rehospitalization RR 0.87 (0.70–1.09) — **not significant**. Safety (AKI, hypotension, hypoglycemia, UTI, SAE) comparable |
| TSA | Firm evidence for mortality reduction; worsening HF not yet firm |
| Status | Peer-reviewed meta-analysis. **Class-level (SGLT2i), not dapagliflozin-specific**; short follow-up; mortality signal driven partly by smaller open-label trials |

DOI 10.1016/j.jchf.2026.103232. PMID 42547169. Published 2026-08-01. Xue X et al. Front Cardiovasc Med 2026. DOI 10.3389/fcvm.2026.1816804. PMID 42205792. [post hoc, exploratory, class-level]

## 4. DAPA-MI — post-MI without diabetes or HF

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Registration | NCT04564742 |
| Design | Registry-based (SWEDEHEART/NICOR), randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled; dapagliflozin 10 mg/d; ~1 yr treatment |
| Population | 4017 patients with acute MI, impaired LV systolic function or Q-wave MI, **without** prior diabetes or chronic HF |
| Primary endpoint | Hierarchical composite (win ratio): death, HHF, nonfatal MI, AF/flutter, new T2DM, NYHA class at last visit, ≥5% body-weight decrease |
| Primary result [RCT-primary] | Win ratio 1.34 (95% CI 1.20–1.50), P<0.001 — **driven mainly by added cardiometabolic components** (new-onset T2DM, weight), analysis method changed mid-trial due to low event accrual |
| Hard outcomes [RCT-secondary] | CV death or HHF: 2.5% (50/2019) vs 2.6% (52/1998); HR 0.95 (0.64–1.40) — **no benefit on hard clinical endpoints** |
| Publication | James S et al. NEJM Evid 2024;3:EVIDoa2300286. DOI 10.1056/EVIDoa2300286. PMID 38320489 |
| Status | Peer-reviewed full publication |

Later analyses (2025):
- By baseline LVEF (<50% vs ≥50%), **subgroup**: WR 1.38 (1.21–1.57) vs 1.32 (1.00–1.73), P-interaction 0.76; no effect on CV death/HHF in either stratum. Erlinge D et al. ESC Heart Fail 2025. DOI 10.1002/ehf2.15420. PMID 40958495. [post hoc subgroup — not proof of uniform benefit]
- By glycemic status/BMI, **subanalysis**: new-onset T2DM in prediabetes 10.1% vs 13.1%, HR 0.74 (0.55–0.99); normoglycemia HR 0.40 (0.15–1.03), P-interaction 0.23; NYHA III–IV symptom reduction greater with prediabetes (P-interaction 0.009). Storey RF et al. J Am Heart Assoc 2025. DOI 10.1161/JAHA.124.040327. PMID 40728174. [post hoc][surrogate-adjacent]

## 5. DICTATE-AHF — early dapagliflozin for decongestion in acute HF

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Registration | NCT04298229 |
| Design | Multicenter, **open-label**, randomized; dapagliflozin 10 mg/d within 24 h of presentation vs structured usual care, protocolized IV diuretic titration, to day 5/discharge |
| Population | 240 hypervolemic acute HF (initially T2DM-only, later broadened); eGFR ≥30 |
| Primary endpoint | Diuretic efficiency (cumulative weight change per cumulative loop diuretic dose) |
| Primary result [RCT-primary] | OR 0.65 (95% CI 0.41–1.02), **P=0.06 — missed statistical significance** |
| Secondary [RCT-secondary][surrogate] | Lower cumulative loop diuretic dose (560 vs 800 mg, P=0.006); fewer IV up-titrations; higher 24-h natriuresis (P=0.03) and urine output (P=0.005) |
| Safety | No increase in diabetic, renal, or CV safety events |
| Publication | Cox ZL et al. J Am Coll Cardiol 2024. DOI 10.1016/j.jacc.2024.02.009. PMID 38569758 |
| Status | Peer-reviewed full publication. All endpoints are decongestion surrogates — **not clinical-outcome evidence** |

Prespecified analysis: eGFR at randomization (median 53) did not modify diuretic-efficiency effects (interaction P=0.22–0.36); no eGFR decline with dapagliflozin (P=0.89). Brademeyer A et al. Am J Cardiol 2026. DOI 10.1016/j.amjcard.2026.04.028. PMID 42013970. [prespec][surrogate]

## 6. Renal Lifecycle — advanced CKD, dialysis, kidney transplant

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Registration | NCT05374291 |
| Design | Pragmatic, international, investigator-initiated, randomized, **double-blind**, placebo-controlled, event-driven (468 first primary events; 80% power for 25% RRR) |
| Population | ≈1500 planned (imaging-substudy paper states ≈1750): eGFR ≤25, or on HD/PD, or kidney transplant with eGFR ≤45 |
| Primary endpoint | Composite: HF hospitalization, all-cause mortality, or (non-dialysis) kidney failure |
| Status as of 2026-08-27 | **Ongoing — no primary results publication or full results presentation identified by systematic search up to the search end date.** The cardiac-imaging substudy design paper (published Clin Kidney J 2026) still describes cardiovascular protection in this population as "to be established" |
| Publications | Design: Bakker WM et al. Nephrol Dial Transplant 2025. DOI 10.1093/ndt/gfaf046. PMID 40053493. [protocol]. Imaging substudies (STOP-HF-in-PD echo n=100; cardiac MRI n=250): Karsten M et al. Clin Kidney J 2026. DOI 10.1093/ckj/sfaf376. PMID 41551843. [protocol][surrogate endpoints] |

Directly relevant post hoc: DAPA-CKD participants who initiated dialysis during the trial (n=167; 68 dapagliflozin vs 99 placebo; mean eGFR at dialysis start ≈14): all-cause mortality aHR 0.47 (95% CI 0.23–0.98); non-CV mortality aHR 0.27 (0.10–0.73); CV mortality aHR 1.33 (0.40–4.40). Bakker WM et al. Clin Kidney J 2026;19(7):sfag170. DOI 10.1093/ckj/sfag170. [post hoc, post-randomization subset — **observational-grade, hypothesis-generating; not evidence of efficacy on dialysis**]

## 7. DARE-ESKD-2 — dapagliflozin on maintenance dialysis

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Registration | NCT05685394 |
| Design | Prospective, randomized, **open-label, blinded-endpoint** (PROBE), 3 dialysis clinics (Brazil); dapagliflozin 10 mg/d vs standard care, 24 weeks |
| Population | 80 adults on chronic dialysis (mean age 56; 54% male) |
| Primary endpoint [surrogate] | Change in NT-proBNP |
| Primary result [RCT-primary][surrogate] | Adjusted between-group difference in NT-proBNP change −155 pg/ml (95% CI −327 to −33) but **P=0.065 — not statistically significant** (control +13 vs dapagliflozin −109 pg/ml) |
| Secondary | KCCQ clinical summary +7 (P=0.073); total summary +7 (P=0.094); 6MWT distance −0.9 m (P=0.956); echo: no differences |
| Safety | No difference in adverse-event rates |
| Publication | Barreto J et al. Kidney Int Rep 2026. DOI 10.1016/j.ekir.2026.106355. PMID 41970273 |
| Status | Peer-reviewed full publication. **Neutral surrogate trial** — do not cite as either efficacy or futility for hard outcomes |

Program context:
- DARE-ESKD-1 (PK): dapagliflozin in dialysis patients is well tolerated, minimally dialyzable (0.10% of dose recovered in dialysate), non-accumulating (Cmax 117 vs 97.6 ng/ml; accumulation ratio 26.7% vs 9.5%). Barreto J et al. Clin J Am Soc Nephrol 2023. DOI 10.2215/CJN.0000000000000196. PMID 37227937. [mech/PK]
- Design: Barreto J et al. Expert Opin Drug Saf 2025. DOI 10.1080/14740338.2024.2412228. PMID 39377184. [protocol]
- Editorial: Moloney BM, Mc Causland FR. Kidney Int Rep 2026. DOI 10.1016/j.ekir.2026.106494. [author interpretation]

## 8. INFINITI — kidney transplant recipients (mechanistic)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Registration | NCT04965935 |
| Design | Mechanistic, randomized, **double-blind**, placebo-controlled; dapagliflozin 10 mg/d vs placebo; 12 weeks; assessments baseline/wk 1/wk 12 |
| Population | 52 stable kidney transplant recipients >6 months post-transplant (mean age 53; 79% male; 57% T2DM; mean eGFR ≈68) |
| Primary endpoint [surrogate] | Seated systolic BP |
| Primary result [RCT-primary][surrogate] | **No significant SBP reduction**: wk 1 −4.4 mmHg (95% CI −10.3 to 1.6, P=0.15); wk 12 −2.9 mmHg (−8.9 to 3.1, P=0.33). Secondary: mean arterial pressure at wk 1 nominally lower with dapagliflozin, −3.9 mmHg (−7.5 to −0.2) [RCT-secondary, per methods-audit extraction 2026-08-27] |
| Mechanistic findings [mech] | Acute iohexol-measured GFR dip preserved: wk 1 −4.2 (−7.14 to −1.24), wk 12 −3.49 (−6.33 to −0.64) mL/min/1.73 m²; glucosuria and 24-h urine volume increased; natriuresis muted |
| Safety | No UTI, genital infection, hypoglycemia, volume depletion, AKI, or rejection; tacrolimus levels stable |
| Publication | Sridhar VS et al. Clin J Am Soc Nephrol 2026 (online 2025-12-12). PMID 41385300 |
| Status | Peer-reviewed publication. **Mechanistic/safety only — provides no clinical-outcome evidence in transplantation** |

## 9. ADPKD

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Only RCT found | Uchiyama K et al. Open-label, randomized, controlled, **crossover** trial of dapagliflozin added to tolvaptan. Kidney Int Rep 2025;10(4):1063–1075. DOI 10.1016/j.ekir.2025.01.023. PMID 40303212 (erratum: 10.1016/j.ekir.2025.05.025) |
| Design/N | n=27 ADPKD patients on tolvaptan; dapagliflozin 10 mg vs usual care, 6-month periods, counterbalanced crossover |
| Primary result [RCT-primary][surrogate] | eGFR slope (months 1–6, creatinine–cystatin C): +2.57 vs −5.65 mL/min/1.73 m²/yr with vs without dapagliflozin, P=0.002; TKV change −0.44% vs +5.04%, P=0.01 |

| Supporting observational | (a) Retrospective tolvaptan-treated cohort, n=48: chronic eGFR slope −1.72 vs −2.30, P=0.595 — neutral. Nakajima R et al. Clin Kidney J 2025. DOI 10.1093/ckj/sfaf269.
