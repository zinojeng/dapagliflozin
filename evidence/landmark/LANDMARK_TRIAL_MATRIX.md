## 2. DECLARE-TIMI 58 (Wiviott SD et al., NEJM 2019; PMID 30415602; DOI 10.1056/NEJMoa1812389; NCT01730534)

**PICO**
- P: 17,160 adults with type 2 diabetes, HbA1c 6.5–12.0%, with established ASCVD (6,974; 40.6%) or multiple risk factors without ASCVD (10,186; 59.4%). Creatinine clearance ≥60 mL/min required at entry (source: Mosenzon 2019, PMID 31196815).
- I: dapagliflozin 10 mg once daily.
- C: matching placebo.
- O: dual primary efficacy — MACE (CV death, MI, ischemic stroke) AND composite of CV death or HHF. Primary safety: MACE noninferiority.

**Key inclusion/exclusion**
- Inclusion: T2D, HbA1c 6.5–12.0%, ASCVD or multiple risk factors.
- Exclusion (renal): creatinine clearance <60 mL/min — the trial population had mostly preserved renal function.
- Baseline eGFR distribution: ≥90: 8,162 (47.6%); 60–<90: 7,732 (45.1%); <60: 1,265 (7.4%) (Mosenzon 2019, PMID 31196815).
- Baseline HF status: patients with HF history could be enrolled; HF prevalence low (~10%) ⚠️FT (full-text baseline table; not re-verified from abstract).

**Sample size and follow-up**: 17,160; median 4.2 years (IQR 3.9–4.4).

**Primary results**
- MACE: 8.8% vs 9.4%; HR 0.93 (95% CI 0.84–1.03); p=0.17 → primary trial result, NOT significant. Noninferiority met (upper CI <1.3, p<0.001).
- CV death or HHF: 4.9% vs 5.8%; HR 0.83 (95% CI 0.73–0.95); p=0.005 → primary trial result, significant.

**Endpoint decomposition (which component drove the composite)**
- HHF: HR 0.73 (0.61–0.88) → drove the benefit.
- CV death: HR 0.98 (0.82–1.17) → neutral.
- Conclusion: the positive composite reflects HHF reduction only; it must NOT be described as a CV-death benefit.

**Secondary outcomes**
- Cardiorenal composite (sustained ≥40% eGFR decline to <60 mL/min/1.73 m², ESRD [dialysis ≥90 d, transplantation, or sustained eGFR <15], or renal/CV death): 4.3% vs 5.6%; HR 0.76 (0.67–0.87) — prespecified secondary outcome; treat as nominal significance until its position in the hierarchical-testing sequence is confirmed from full text/SAP (methods audit P-2).
- Renal-specific composite (excluding CV death): HR 0.53 (0.43–0.66); p<0.0001 (Mosenzon 2019).
- Sustained ≥40% eGFR decline to <60: 1.4% vs 2.6%; HR 0.54 (0.43–0.67) (Mosenzon 2019).
- ESRD or renal death: 0.1% (11) vs 0.3% (27); HR 0.41 (0.20–0.82); p=0.012 (Mosenzon 2019) — note very low event counts.
- All-cause death: 6.2% vs 6.6%; HR 0.93 (0.82–1.04) — neutral.

**Subgroup interpretation**
- Renal composites consistent across baseline eGFR (p-interaction 0.97/0.87) and ASCVD status (p-interaction 0.67/0.72) (Mosenzon 2019) — prespecified subgroups, consistency claims only.
- eGFR trajectory: larger initial decline with dapagliflozin at 6 months; equalized by 2 years; less decline than placebo at 3–4 years (Mosenzon 2019).
- Obesity analysis (Oyama 2022, PMID 34427295, DOI 10.1093/eurheartj/ehab530): relative effects consistent across BMI (p-int ≥0.20); larger absolute HHF reduction in obese — post hoc, hypothesis-generating.

**Safety (from primary report)**
- DKA: 0.3% vs 0.1%; p=0.02 — more common with dapagliflozin.
- Genital infections leading to discontinuation or serious AE: 0.9% vs 0.1%; p<0.001.
- No significant excess in amputation, fracture, volume depletion, or hypersensitivity ⚠️FT (full-text safety table; not re-verified from abstract).

**Applicability limits**
- Excluded CrCl <60 → results do not characterize moderate–severe CKD (DAPA-CKD covers this).
- Largest primary-prevention-leaning SGLT2i CVOT (59.4% without established ASCVD) — MACE effect in the multiple-risk-factor stratum was null in class-level meta-analysis (Zelniker 2019, below).
- Low baseline HF prevalence; HHF benefit is about incident/recurrent HF hospitalization in a diabetes population, not an HF-treatment claim.

## 3. DAPA-HF (McMurray JJV et al., NEJM 2019; PMID 31535829; DOI 10.1056/NEJMoa1911303; NCT03036124)

**PICO**
- P: 4,744 adults with chronic HF, NYHA II–IV, LVEF ≤40%, elevated NT-proBNP, on recommended therapy; with and without T2D (2,605 [55%] without diabetes; Petrie 2020, PMID 32219386).
- I: dapagliflozin 10 mg once daily added to recommended therapy.
- C: placebo.
- O: composite of worsening HF (hospitalization or urgent visit resulting in IV therapy for HF) or CV death.

**Key inclusion/exclusion**
- Inclusion: NYHA II–IV, LVEF ≤40%, elevated NT-proBNP.
- Exclusion (renal): eGFR <30 mL/min/1.73 m² ⚠️FT (full-text methods; not re-verified from abstract).
- Exclusion: SBP <95 mmHg, recent symptomatic hypotension ⚠️FT; type 1 diabetes ⚠️FT.

**Sample size and follow-up**: 4,744; median 18.2 months.

**Primary result**
- 386/2,373 (16.3%) vs 502/2,371 (21.2%); HR 0.74 (95% CI 0.65–0.85); p<0.001 — primary trial result, hard clinical endpoints.

**Endpoint decomposition**
- First worsening HF event: 10.0% vs 13.7%; HR 0.70 (0.59–0.83).
- CV death: 9.6% vs 11.5%; HR 0.82 (0.69–0.98) — individually significant.
- All-cause death: 11.6% vs 13.9%; HR 0.83 (0.71–0.97).
- Conclusion: in HFrEF both components moved; this is the strongest mortality signal among the four landmark trials at single-trial level.

**Diabetes subgroup (Petrie 2020, JAMA; PMID 32219386; DOI 10.1001/jama.2020.1906 — labeled exploratory analysis)**
- Without diabetes: 13.2% vs 17.7%; HR 0.73 (0.60–0.88).
- With diabetes: 20.0% vs 25.5%; HR 0.75 (0.63–0.90); p-interaction = 0.80.
- Without diabetes, HbA1c <5.7%: HR 0.67 (0.47–0.96); HbA1c ≥5.7%: HR 0.74 (0.59–0.94); p-interaction = 0.72.
- Interpretation discipline: consistency across strata; do NOT claim proven equal efficacy — the correct claim is "no evidence of treatment-effect modification by glycemic status."

**Safety**
- Volume depletion AE: 7.3% vs 6.1% (no diabetes), 7.8% vs 7.8% (diabetes) (Petrie 2020).
- Frequencies of volume depletion, renal dysfunction, and hypoglycemia AEs did not differ between arms (primary report).

**Applicability limits**
- Excludes eGFR <30, hypotensive, and hemodynamically unstable patients; results apply to ambulatory chronic HFrEF, not acute decompensation (in-hospital initiation is frontier-domain: DAPA ACT HF-TIMI 68).

## 4. DELIVER (Solomon SD et al., NEJM 2022; PMID 36027570; DOI 10.1056/NEJMoa2206286; NCT03619213)

**PICO**
- P: 6,263 adults with HF and LVEF >40% (HFmrEF/HFpEF), with or without T2D; eGFR ≥25 required (Mc Causland 2023, PMID 36326604); mean age 71.7 ± 9.6 y (Peikert 2022, PMID 36029467).
- I: dapagliflozin 10 mg once daily added to usual therapy.
- C: placebo.
- O: worsening HF (unplanned HF hospitalization or urgent HF visit) or CV death.

**Sample size and follow-up**: 6,263; median 2.3 years (IQR 1.7–2.8).

**Primary result**
- 512/3,131 (16.4%) vs 610/3,132 (19.5%); HR 0.82 (95% CI 0.73–0.92); p<0.001.

**Endpoint decomposition**
- Worsening HF: 11.8% vs 14.5%; HR 0.79 (0.69–0.91) → drove the composite.
- CV death: 7.4% vs 8.3%; HR 0.88 (0.74–1.05) → not individually significant.
- Total events and symptom burden lower with dapagliflozin.
- Conclusion: in HFmrEF/HFpEF, the demonstrated single-trial benefit is chiefly prevention of worsening-HF events; mortality claims rest on pooled analyses (below), not on DELIVER alone.

**Prespecified subgroups**
- Consistent for LVEF ≥60% vs <60%; with/without diabetes (primary report).
- Age (Peikert 2022, Circ Heart Fail; PMID 36029467; DOI 10.1161/CIRCHEARTFAILURE.122.010080): consistent across <55 to ≥75 y (41.4% were ≥75); p-interaction 0.95; safety similar across age.
- Kidney function (Mc Causland 2023, JAMA Cardiol; PMID 36326604; DOI 10.1001/jamacardio.2022.4210; prespecified): 49% eGFR <60; primary-outcome effect not modified by eGFR category (p-int=0.16). Post hoc kidney composite NOT reduced: HR 1.08 (0.79–1.49), event rate low (1.1/100 pt-y); eGFR slope decline attenuated (month 1–36 difference 1.4 [1.0–1.8] mL/min/1.73 m²/y; p<0.001) — surrogate endpoint, not a clinical kidney-outcome benefit.
- Frailty (Butt 2022, Circulation; PMID 36029465; DOI 10.1161/CIRCULATIONAHA.122.061754; prespecified): FI class 1/2/3 HRs 0.85 (0.68–1.06), 0.89 (0.74–1.08), 0.74 (0.61–0.91); p-interaction 0.40 → relative effect consistent. KCCQ-OSS improvement at 4 months larger with greater frailty (0.3 vs 1.5 vs 3.4 points; p-int 0.021). Adverse reactions and discontinuation more frequent with greater frailty but NOT more common on dapagliflozin than placebo within each frailty class.

**Safety**: overall AE incidence similar between groups (primary report).

**Applicability limits**
- Ambulatory or hospitalization-stabilized HF with LVEF >40%; eGFR <25 excluded.
- CV-death and all-cause-death benefits should not be attributed to DELIVER alone.

## 5. DAPA-CKD (Heerspink HJL et al., NEJM 2020; PMID 32970396; DOI 10.1056/NEJMoa2024816; NCT03036150)

**PICO**
- P: 4,304 adults with CKD, eGFR 25–75 mL/min/1.73 m², UACR 200–5000 mg/g, on standard care; 2,906 (67.5–68%) with T2D (Wheeler 2021, PMID 33338413; Heerspink 2021, PMID 34619108); mean age 62 (SD 12); 33.1% women.
- I: dapagliflozin 10 mg once daily.
- C: placebo.
- O: composite of sustained ≥50% eGFR decline, ESKD, or death from renal or CV causes.

**Key inclusion/exclusion**
- Inclusion: eGFR 25–75, UACR 200–5000 mg/g (albuminuric CKD), stable maximally tolerated ACEi/ARB unless contraindicated ⚠️FT.
- Exclusion: type 1 diabetes, polycystic kidney disease, lupus nephritis, ANCA-associated vasculitis, recent immunosuppression ⚠️FT (full-text methods; not re-verified from abstract). → ADPKD efficacy is NOT covered by DAPA-CKD.

**Sample size and follow-up**: 4,304; median 2.4 years (IQR 2.0–2.7); trial stopped early on DMC recommendation for efficacy.

**Primary result**
- 197/2,152 (9.2%) vs 312/2,152 (14.5%); HR 0.61 (95% CI 0.51–0.72); p<0.001; NNT 19 (95% CI 15–27).

**Endpoint decomposition / secondary outcomes**
- Kidney-specific composite (≥50% eGFR decline, ESKD, renal death): HR 0.56 (0.45–0.68); p<0.001 → kidney events drove the composite.
- CV death or HHF: HR 0.71 (0.55–0.92); p=0.009.
- All-cause death: 4.7% vs 6.8%; HR 0.69 (0.53–0.88); p=0.004.

**Subgroup interpretation**
- Diabetes status (Wheeler 2021, Lancet Diabetes Endocrinol; PMID 33338413; DOI 10.1016/S2213-8587(20)30369-7; prespecified): T2D HR 0.64 (0.52–0.79); no diabetes HR 0.50 (0.35–0.72); p-interaction 0.24 → consistent; benefit is not glucose-dependent.
- CKD etiology (Wheeler 2021): diabetic nephropathy 0.63 (0.51–0.78); glomerulonephritides 0.43 (0.26–0.71); ischemic/hypertensive 0.75 (0.44–1.26); other/unknown 0.58 (0.29–1.19); p-interaction 0.53 — subgroup consistency, some strata underpowered.
- Stage 4 CKD (Chertow 2021, JASN; PMID 34272327; DOI 10.1681/ASN.2021020167; prespecified): 624 participants with baseline eGFR <30 (293 dapagliflozin / 331 placebo). Primary endpoint reduction 27% (95% CI −2 to 47) — CI crosses null; p-interaction vs stages 2/3 = 0.22; eGFR slope −2.15 vs −3.38 mL/min/1.73 m²/y (p=0.005); safety comparable. Interpretation: consistent-with-overall, but stage 4 alone is not independently significant — do not quote "proven benefit in eGFR <30" from this subgroup.
- eGFR slope (Heerspink 2021, Lancet Diabetes Endocrinol; PMID 34619108; DOI 10.1016/S2213-8587(21)00242-4; prespecified): total slope difference 0.95 (0.63–1.27) mL/min/1.73 m²/y favoring dapagliflozin; acute dip at 2 weeks 2.61 (T2D) and 2.01 (non-T2D) mL/min/1.73 m²; chronic slope benefit larger in T2D (2.26 vs 1.29 mL/min/1.73 m²/y; p-int 0.0049). The initial dip is expected and hemodynamic; slope is a surrogate supporting, not replacing, the hard-endpoint result.

**Safety**: known safety profile confirmed; serious AEs/discontinuations similar by diabetes status (Wheeler 2021).

**Applicability limits**
- Albuminuric CKD only (UACR ≥200 mg/g). Non-albuminuric CKD is NOT represented — guideline extensions there rest on other evidence.
- eGFR <25, dialysis, transplant, ADPKD, and immune glomerulonephritis on immunosuppression are outside the trial.

## 6. Major pooled analyses (evidence tier: prespecified pooled/meta-analyses)

### 6.1 DAPA-HF + DELIVER patient-level pooled (Jhund PS et al., Nat Med 2022; PMID 36030328; DOI 10.1038/s41591-022-01971-4; PROSPERO CRD42022346524; prespecified)
- N = 11,007; mean LVEF 44% (SD 14).
- CV death: HR 0.86 (0.76–0.97); p=0.01.
- All-cause death: HR 0.90 (0.82–0.99); p=0.03.
- Total HF admissions: rate ratio 0.71 (0.65–0.78); p<0.001.
- MACE: HR 0.90 (0.81–1.00); p=0.045.
- No evidence effect differs by ejection fraction.
- Role: this is the correct citation for a dapagliflozin mortality claim across the EF spectrum — not DELIVER alone.

### 6.2 Cause-specific mortality pooled (Desai AS et al., JAMA Cardiol 2022; PMID 36189985; DOI 10.1001/jamacardio.2022.3736; prespecified secondary)
- 1,628 deaths; 53.5% CV, 29.9% non-CV, 16.5% undetermined; non-CV proportion higher at higher EF.
- CV death HR 0.86 (0.75–0.98); sudden death HR 0.84 (0.70–1.01; p=0.07); HF death HR 0.88 (0.70–1.11); little difference for stroke/MI death.

### 6.3 SGLT2i in HF, five-trial meta-analysis (Vaduganathan M et al., Lancet 2022; PMID 36041474; DOI 10.1016/S0140-6736(22)01429-5; prespecified; class-level, trial-level data)
- DELIVER + EMPEROR-Preserved (n=12,251): CV death/first HHF HR 0.80 (0.73–0.87); CV death 0.88 (0.77–1.00); first HHF 0.74 (0.67–0.83).
- All five trials (+DAPA-HF, EMPEROR-Reduced, SOLOIST-WHF; n=21,947): CV death/HHF 0.77 (0.72–0.82); CV death 0.87 (0.79–0.95); first HHF 0.72 (0.67–0.78); all-cause death 0.92 (0.86–0.99). Consistent across 14 subgroups incl. EF.
- Note: class-level evidence; includes non-dapagliflozin trials.

### 6.4 SGLT2i kidney-outcome collaborative meta-analysis (Nuffield Dept of Population Health Renal Studies Group / SMART-C, Lancet 2022; PMID 36351458; DOI 10.1016/S0140-6736(22)02074-8)
- 13 trials, 90,409 analyzed (82.7% diabetes; 17.3% without); trial-level mean baseline eGFR range 37–85.
- Kidney disease progression (standardized: sustained ≥50% eGFR decline, sustained low eGFR, ESKD, kidney-failure death): RR 0.63 (0.58–0.69), similar with/without diabetes.
- Acute kidney injury: RR 0.77 (0.70–0.84).
- CV death or HHF: RR 0.77 (0.74–0.81).
- CV death: RR 0.86 (0.81–0.92); non-CV death 0.94 (0.88–1.02).
- Absolute benefits outweighed serious hazards of ketoacidosis/amputation (authors' assessment).
- Note: class-level evidence.

### 6.5 SGLT2i CVOT meta-analysis in T2D (Zelniker TA et al., Lancet 2019; PMID 30424892; DOI 10.1016/S0140-6736(18)32590-X)
- 3 trials (EMPA-REG OUTCOME, CANVAS, DECLARE-TIMI 58), n=34,322; 60.2% with ASCVD.
- MACE: HR 0.89 (0.83–0.96); benefit confined to established ASCVD (0.86 [0.80–0.93]) vs none in multiple-risk-factor-only patients (1.00 [0.87–1.16]); p-interaction 0.0501.
- CV death/HHF: 0.77 (0.71–0.84) regardless of ASCVD or HF history.
- Renal progression: 0.55 (0.48–0.64) regardless of ASCVD.
- Note: class-level; underpins the "MACE only in secondary prevention, HF/renal benefit everywhere" teaching point — but the interaction is borderline (p=0.0501) and the evidence is class-level/indirect: state as hypothesis-consistent stratification, NOT a proven subgroup confinement (methods audit O-4).
